How to play

Basics. Tap anywhere (or Space) to jump between orbits. Dodge red blocks, collect gold stars. Stars in a row build a streak that multiplies their value up to ×4.

Close calls. Switch at the very last moment before a block for bonus points.

Faded blocks. Dashed purple blocks are harmless while faded. Fly through for a bonus, but watch for the flicker before they turn solid.

Power-ups. Shield, Slow-mo, Magnet, 2×. Have two at once for a combo, e.g. Magnet + 2× = Golden Stars.

Sectors & twists. Every 25 seconds you enter a new sector with a random twist: Blackout, Mirror World, Hyperdrive, Triple Orbit and more. On three orbits, tap left to move in and right to move out.

Comets. A warning shows which orbit. Be on the other one when it passes.

Bosses. Every 3rd sector. Grab purple shards to damage them within 22 seconds. Each of the 6 bosses has its own attacks.

Abilities. Stars, close calls and dodges charge the button in the bottom-right. Tap it (or press E) to fire your equipped ability. Unlock more in the shop.

Ghost race. Your best endless run plays back as a faint dashed dot. The top bar shows how far ahead or behind you are. Turn it off in Settings.

Boons. At the start of an endless run and after every boss, pick 1 of 3 perks. They stack.

Progress. Earn stardust for skins and upgrades, complete missions and trophies, hit score goals to unlock special cosmetics, and prestige (up to P10) for permanent bonuses, exclusive rewards and ✪ tokens for the prestige tree.
